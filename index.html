Media Bias Detection in Political News

CSC-680 Introduction to Data Mining, American University

Text classifier trained on 40,945 articles from 91 outlets using TF-IDF features and class-balanced logistic regression (ROC-AUC 0.82). Outlet names were stripped so the model learned writing style, not source identity. The highest-scoring outlets spanned the political spectrum, suggesting the model picks up opinionated versus neutral writing.

Fall 2025

Your Body, Their Data: Digital Surveillance and the New Frontier of Reproductive Control

Policy brief, GOVT-620 Applied Politics & American Public Policy, American University

Diagnoses how state trigger bans, an unregulated data broker industry, and algorithmic inference have turned location history and period-tracking apps into evidence against people seeking reproductive care, and why HIPAA does not stop it. Drawing on case law, reported prosecutions, and a technical risk assessment, it proposes the Digital Bodyguard Act: a prohibited-by-default federal standard with FTC enforcement and a private right of action. This brief became the basis for the published article.

Mar 2025

The Backlash Equation: Rising Women's Employment + Male Resentment = Authoritarian Gains

GOVT-650 Political Analysis, American University (presented April 22, 2025)

Women now earn most college and graduate degrees and make up a growing share of the workforce, yet 2024 produced some of the widest gender gaps in vote choice on record. This paper asks whether men's political shift tracks women's economic gains, designing a time-series cross-sectional study that tests whether rising women's labor force participation predicts Republican vote share among men. It uses BLS labor force data (1948 to 2025) and national exit polls (1980 to 2024), with controls for male unemployment, education, and religiosity.
