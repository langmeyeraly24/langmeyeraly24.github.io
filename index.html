Voter Targeting and Partisan Scoring for a Georgia U.S. Senate Campaign

GOVT-520 Campaign Management Institute · Python, Excel, Quarto

County-level targeting model built from six election cycles (2018 to 2024), May 2026 registration, and turnout data for all 159 Georgia counties. Sized a mobilization universe of 602,753 low-propensity Republicans and a persuasion universe of about 356,606 split-ticket voters, set vote targets at 50%+1 and 52%, and benchmarked the model against eight Collins vs. Ossoff polls.

Apr 2026

Media Bias Detection in Political News

CSC-680 Introduction to Data Mining · Python, scikit-learn

Text classifier trained on 40,945 articles from 91 outlets using TF-IDF features and class-balanced logistic regression (ROC-AUC 0.82). Outlet names were stripped so the model learned writing style, not source identity. The highest-scoring outlets spanned the political spectrum, suggesting the model picks up opinionated versus neutral writing.
